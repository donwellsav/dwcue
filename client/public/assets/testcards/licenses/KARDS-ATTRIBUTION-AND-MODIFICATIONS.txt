DonWells Cue modifications:
- Ported the test-card components and renderer surface from the standalone
  Kards Electron application into the DonWells Cue Vue 3/Nuxt client.
- Replaced visible Alteka/Kards branding and credits with DonWells Cue and
  dwcue.com while retaining the upstream test geometry, levels and layout.
- Removed the standalone Kards IPC, keyboard, export, wallpaper, audio-device
  selection and control-window shell.
- Replaced the nine autonomous browser video/audio elements with one named
  audio-sync slot for DonWells Cue's native-clocked, muted video output.
- Replaced viewport-global measurement and resize hooks with surface-local
  ResizeObserver measurement and deterministic teardown.
- Vendored the vue3-resize-text 0.1.0 sizing behavior and added element-local
  observation plus teardown of delayed work.
- Scoped Font Awesome Free styling to the test-card surface.
- Modified particles.js 2.0.0 teardown so its resize listener is removable and
  its global instance registry remains reusable during local reinitialization.
- Preserved upstream diagnostic-motion semantics: the animation option controls
  only signals originally bound to it, while Deghost and Audio Sync continue
  their diagnostic loops independently.
