========================================================================
  DonWells Cue — READ ME FIRST (first launch on macOS, one-time)
========================================================================

This build is not signed with an Apple Developer certificate yet,
so macOS shows a one-time security warning the first time you open
the app. That is expected — the app is fine. Getting past it takes
about 30 seconds, once:

  1.  Double-click "DonWells Cue". macOS shows
      "Apple could not verify DonWells Cue is free of malware".
      Click  Done  (the app will not open yet — that's normal).

  2.  Open  System Settings → Privacy & Security.

  3.  Scroll to the bottom. You'll see
      "DonWells Cue was blocked ..."
      Click  Open Anyway  next to it.

  4.  Click  Open  in the confirmation, then approve with
      Touch ID or your password.

Done — the app now opens normally every time. You never repeat this.

  Windows: if SmartScreen appears, click "More info" → "Run anyway".

  Full guide:  https://dwcue.com/#download
  Source:      https://github.com/donwellsav/dwcue
========================================================================
